Learning paths

Start where people actually get hurt online

Each path is built for plain-English education, not fear, jargon, or technical gatekeeping.

Path 01

Phishing & scams

Teach people how to slow down, inspect messages, verify requests, and report scams before damage is done.

Path 02

Privacy literacy

Show what personal data is worth, how platforms profile users, and how to reduce unnecessary exposure.

Path 03

Social engineering

Cover urgency, authority pressure, impersonation, emotional manipulation, and common pretexting tactics.

Path 04

Safe AI use

Help people recognize synthetic content, verify claims, and understand how AI changes fraud and trust.

Path 05

Digital wellness

Balance security with sustainable habits so safety does not become burnout, anxiety, or constant vigilance.

Path 06

Digital autonomy

Promote confident use of privacy settings, safer defaults, open tools, and independent decision-making online.

About

Cyber Shield is the hub. The detector is one feature.

Created by GT Bailey, cybersecurity content creator focused on practical digital safety without fear, jargon, or data collection.

Cyber Shield is being repositioned as a public-facing education and digital literacy platform: awareness media, practical guides, scam prevention, privacy education, AI safety, and healthier online habits.

The scanning utility stays available for suspicious links, screenshots, and QR codes, but it no longer needs to carry the entire identity of the site.
