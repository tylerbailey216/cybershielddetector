Categorized Learning Paths

Structured ways to learn the parts of digital life that matter most

Each path is built for plain-English education, not fear, jargon, or technical gatekeeping.

Path 01

Phishing & scams

Teach people how to slow down, inspect messages, verify requests, and report scams before damage is done.

Path 02

Privacy literacy

Show what personal data is worth, how platforms profile users, and how to reduce unnecessary exposure.

Path 03

Social engineering

Cover urgency, authority pressure, impersonation, emotional manipulation, and common pretexting tactics.

Path 04

Safe AI use

Help people recognize synthetic content, verify claims, and understand how AI changes fraud and trust.

Path 05

Digital wellness

Balance security with sustainable habits so safety does not become burnout, anxiety, or constant vigilance.

Path 06

Digital autonomy

Promote confident use of privacy settings, safer defaults, open tools, and independent decision-making online.
