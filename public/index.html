About Cyber Shield

An independent digital awareness publication

Created by GT Bailey with a practical voice that favors clarity, usefulness, and real-world digital self-defense over fear or technical posturing.

Created by GT Bailey

Cyber Shield is a magazine-style digital literacy project focused on scams, privacy tradeoffs, synthetic trust, and everyday online self-defense.

The editorial stance is simple: technology should help people, not confuse them, corner them, or quietly take more than it gives back.
